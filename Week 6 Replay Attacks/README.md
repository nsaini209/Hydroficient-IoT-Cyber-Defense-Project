# Week 6 - Replay Attacks

### This week’s focus was on establishing a multi-layered defense to protect the water sensor and publisher from Replay Attacks. To ensure the integrity and freshness of every transmission, I implemented three distinct security controls: Timestamps, Sequence Counters, and HMAC (Hash-based Message Authentication Code). Timestamps ensure that data is processed within a specific time window to prevent the injection of delayed packets, while Sequence Counters assign a unique, incrementing ID to every log entry, allowing the system to immediately identify and discard duplicate messages. Beyond these temporal controls, I integrated HMAC signatures to provide robust message authentication and data integrity. By signing each sensor reading with a unique cryptographic signature, the system can verify that the data originated from a trusted source and remained untampered during transit. This combination of mTLS for secure transport and application-layer defenses effectively hardens the IoT infrastructure against sophisticated interception and re-transmission attempts.
